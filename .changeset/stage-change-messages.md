Mention a stage in the composer with Thread stages, such as `@Blocked`, to ask the agent to move the thread there. Ribbon messages a thread with "Thread stage updated: @Idle → @Blocked" when you or another thread change its stage. Turn the messages off with **Message threads when their stage changes** in Ribbon settings.
