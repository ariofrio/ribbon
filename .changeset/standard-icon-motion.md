Standardized heading icons open and shut the way a book and a folder do. The
book lies on its back and its cover turns over on the spine, with half the
pages, to lie open beside the rest; the folder's front falls forward from its
fold and stands back up. Both are drawn in the icon style from a small 3D
model, one size throughout, with whatever a nearer part covers hidden.
